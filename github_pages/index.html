RIPPLO A messenger that works without internet access.

Ripplo is a free, open-source messenger that does not require internet access, a SIM card or an account. Your phone connects to nearby phones, and encrypted messages hop from phone to phone until they reach the recipient.

How it works

Phones talk to phones. Without any intermediary server.

Ordinary messengers send everything through company servers, but Ripplo bypasses that. Your phone connects directly to the phones around it that have the Ripplo app.

Add a friend in person

Scan their QR code or send an invite link. Your secret keys are created on your phone and never leave it.

Write a message

If your friend is nearby, the message goes straight to them. If not, other phones with Ripplo along the way will pass it on.

Only your friend can read it

Messages are encrypted on your phone and decrypted only on your friend's. Phones in between see only encrypted, unreadable data.

Good to know: the more people use Ripplo nearby, the farther your messages can travel. Ripplo is an early version, so expect minor imperfections.

Why Ripplo

A private chat for when the network is gone.

Works without internet

No mobile signal, Wi-Fi router or SIM card needed. Ideal in the mountains, at festivals, during blackouts and while traveling.

No account or phone number

You only choose a name. There is no registration and no intermediary company.

Encrypted end to end

Ripplo uses the Signal protocol, the same kind of encryption trusted by the most secure messengers, with additional protection against future quantum computers.

Intermediaries relaying messages see nothing

Phones that pass your message along cannot read it and do not know who wrote it.

More than text

Group chats, voice notes, photos and videos up to 20 MB. On Android it also works with the screen off.

Free and open

No ads, subscriptions or tracking. Anyone can read the code and check that it does what we promise.

Supported connections

Five ways to reach the phone next to you.

Ripplo can use several connections at once. It regularly checks how fast each of them is and picks the fastest and most stable one, so you do not have to choose anything manually.

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)

Low-power Bluetooth. Finds nearby phones and saves battery. It is suitable for bigger things, like photos and voice notes; it switches to a faster Bluetooth channel (L2CAP). Works between Android and iOS.

AndroidiOS

Wi-Fi network (LAN)

When phones are on the same Wi-Fi network or on one of their hotspots, they find each other, which allows them to exchange encrypted packets.

AndroidiOS

Wi-Fi Direct

Two Android phones create their own private Wi-Fi connection, fast and without a router.

AndroidiOS

Wi-Fi Aware

A newer, faster way for Android phones to find each other and talk. Requires a phone that supports it.

Android 8+iOS

Apple Multipeer

Apple's own technology for communication between nearby devices, used between iPhones and iPads.

AndroidiOS

The security behind it

The Signal protocol (libsignal) with PQXDH and the SPQR triple ratchet for post-quantum protection. Sealed sender hides who wrote a message. Your private keys never leave your phone.

Protection against tampering: every release ships with a SHA256SUMS file, and the Android app verifies its own signature before it updates itself.

Questions

Plain answers.

Is there a messenger that works without internet?

Yes. Ripplo is a free, open-source messenger that needs no internet, no mobile signal, no SIM card and no account. Phones connect directly to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and every phone with Ripplo can pass messages along.

How does communication in a mesh network work?

In a mesh network every phone is both a sender and a helper. If your friend is too far away to connect directly, your message hops through other phones with Ripplo until it reaches its destination. The phones in between only pass on encrypted data and cannot read it.

Is Ripplo private and secure?

Yes. Messages, voice notes, photos and videos are end-to-end encrypted with Signal's official libsignal library, with post-quantum protection (PQXDH and the SPQR triple ratchet). Phones in between cannot read messages or see who wrote them. Private keys stay on your phone.

Do I need a phone number or an account?

No. You only choose a name. There are no servers, no registration and no phone numbers. You add friends in person, with a QR code or by sending an invite link.

What technologies does Ripplo use to connect phones?

Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE, with a fast L2CAP channel), LAN over the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct and Wi-Fi Aware on Android, and Apple Multipeer Connectivity on iPhone.

Is Ripplo free?

Yes. Ripplo is completely free, has no ads or subscriptions, and its source code is open under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Which phones does Ripplo support?

Android and iPhone. These phones can talk to each other over Bluetooth and LAN. The Android app is a signed APK file. The iPhone version is currently an unsigned IPA file that you install using a tool for installing from outside the App Store.

When is an offline messenger useful?

At festivals and concerts where networks are overloaded, during hikes or trips without signal, during blackouts and natural disasters, in places with weak or restricted internet, or when you want to talk to people nearby without another company's server in between.
