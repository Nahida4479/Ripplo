RIPPLO The messenger that works without internet.

Ripplo is a free, open-source chat app. No internet, no SIM card, no account. Your phone talks to nearby phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and your messages hop from phone to phone until they arrive. Everything is private.

How it works

Phones talk to phones. Nobody in the middle.

Normal messengers send everything through a company's servers on the internet. Ripplo skips that. Your phone connects directly to the phones around it.

Add a friend in person

Scan their QR code or share an invite link. Your secret keys are created on your phone and never leave it.

Write a message

If your friend is close, it goes straight to them. If not, other Ripplo phones in between carry it forward.

Only your friend can read it

Messages are locked on your phone and unlocked only on theirs. Phones in the middle just see sealed, unreadable data.

Good to know: the more people use Ripplo around you, the farther your messages can travel. Ripplo is an early version, so expect a few rough edges.

Why Ripplo

Private chat for when the network is gone.

Works with no internet

No mobile signal, no Wi-Fi router, no SIM card needed. Perfect for mountains, festivals, blackouts and travel.

No account, no phone number

You only choose a name. There is no sign-up and no company that knows who your friends are.

Locked end to end

Uses the Signal protocol, the same kind of encryption trusted by top secure messengers, with extra protection against future quantum computers.

Helpers can't see anything

Phones that pass your message along cannot read it, cannot tell who wrote it, and never show you a notification for it.

More than text

Group chats, voice notes, photos and videos up to 20 MB. Works with the screen off on Android.

Free and open source

No ads, no subscription, no tracking. Anyone can read the code and check that it does what we say.

Supported connections

Six ways to reach the phone next to you.

Ripplo can use several connections at the same time. Every minute it tests how fast each one is and uses the fastest that works, so you never have to choose.

Bluetooth LE

Low-power Bluetooth. Finds nearby phones and keeps battery use small. Works between Android and iPhone.

AndroidiPhone

Bluetooth L2CAP

A faster Bluetooth lane for bigger things like photos and voice notes. Works between Android and iPhone.

Android 10+iPhone

Wi-Fi network (LAN)

When phones share the same Wi-Fi or one phone's hotspot, they find each other and chat quickly. No internet needed.

AndroidiPhone

Wi-Fi Direct

Two Android phones make their own private Wi-Fi link, fast and without a router.

AndroidiPhone

Wi-Fi Aware

A newer, quicker way for Android phones to find and talk to each other. Needs a phone that supports it.

Android 8+iPhone

Apple Multipeer

Apple's own nearby-device technology, used between iPhones and iPads.

The security behind it

Signal protocol (libsignal) with PQXDH and the SPQR triple ratchet for post-quantum protection. Sealed sender hides who wrote a message. Your secret keys never leave your phone.

Checked downloads

Every release ships with a SHA256SUMS file, and the Android app verifies its own signature before it updates itself.

Questions

Plain answers.

Is there a messaging app that works without internet?

Yes. Ripplo is a free, open-source messenger that needs no internet, no mobile signal, no SIM card and no account. Phones connect directly to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, and every phone with Ripplo can pass messages along.

How does mesh messaging work?

In a mesh, every phone is both a sender and a helper. If your friend is too far away to reach directly, your message jumps through other Ripplo phones in between until it arrives. The phones in the middle only forward sealed data and cannot read it.

Is Ripplo private and secure?

Yes. Messages, voice notes, photos and videos are end-to-end encrypted with Signal's official libsignal library, including post-quantum protection (PQXDH and the SPQR triple ratchet). Relay phones cannot read messages and cannot see who wrote them (sealed sender). Secret keys stay on your phone.

Do I need a phone number or an account?

No. You only pick a name. There are no servers, no registration and no phone numbers. You add friends in person with a QR code or by sharing an invite link.

What technologies does Ripplo use to connect phones?

Bluetooth Low Energy, Bluetooth L2CAP, LAN over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct and Wi-Fi Aware on Android, and Apple Multipeer Connectivity on iPhone. Several can run at once and the app always uses the fastest working link.

Is Ripplo free?

Yes. Ripplo is completely free, has no ads and no subscription, and its source code is open under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Which phones does Ripplo support?

Android and iPhone, and they can talk to each other over Bluetooth and LAN. The Android app is a signed APK. The iPhone build is currently an unsigned IPA that you install with a sideloading tool.

When is an offline messenger useful?

At festivals and concerts where networks are overloaded, while hiking or traveling with no signal, during blackouts and natural disasters, in places with weak or restricted internet, or any time you want to talk to people nearby without a company's server in between.
